## Critical: blocks indexing or ranking

1. **Canonical, og:url, sitemap and robots point at `akshitsharma.dev`, but that host
   308-redirects to `www.akshitsharma.dev`.** Google is told the "real" page is a URL that
   redirects away, and the sitemap lists only that redirecting URL. Pick one host (www, since
   it's what serves) and use it in the canonical, og:url, og:image, sitemap `<loc>`,
   robots `Sitemap:` line and every JSON-LD `@id`/`url`.
2. **The site doesn't show up in web search for its own domain or for your name + skills.**
   Searches for `"akshitsharma.dev"` and "Akshit Sharma AI agent developer LangGraph RAG
   Chandigarh" returned other people and Fiverr/Upwork/Contra gigs, not you. Submit the
   (fixed) sitemap in Google Search Console and Bing Webmaster Tools and request indexing.
3. **Mobile speed fails Core Web Vitals.** LCP 7.4 s (good is under 2.5 s), Total Blocking
   Time 2.7 s, Speed Index 10 s, 24.6 s of main-thread work. Main causes:
   - the ~3 s intro loader sits in front of the largest content on every visit;
   - heavy JavaScript (framer-motion, several always-on canvases and rAF loops): 252 KB unused;
   - Google Analytics **and** Google Tag Manager both load (730 ms of third-party blocking).

## High: significantly limits rankings and leads

4. **One single page for everything.** No URL exists for "AI agent development", "AI chatbot
   development", "RAG development", any case study, or any article. Every unknown path
   (`/blog`, `/projects/...`) just returns the homepage, so there's nothing for long-tail
   searches to land on. Build real pages (see plan below).
5. **Soft 404s.** Any missing URL (`/llms.txt`, `/manifest.json`, `/apple-touch-icon.png`,
   typos) returns the homepage with status 200 instead of 404. Google treats this as
   duplicate/soft-404 content.
6. **All content is rendered by JavaScript.** The served HTML has an empty `<div id="root">`;
   only the `<noscript>` summary is crawlable without JS. Google renders JS, but slower and
   less reliably; most AI crawlers (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude) don't run JS at all, so
   they see almost nothing. Pre-render the pages at build time.
7. **Every section heading is printed twice** in the DOM ("Have a Software Task? Hire
   Me.Have a Software Task? Hire Me."), from the split-text reveal animation. Crawlers read
   duplicated headings.
8. **About 45 blocks of text sit at opacity 0** until scrolled into view, and hide again when
   scrolled away (reveal animations with `once: false`). Rendering crawlers may treat it as
   hidden text; it also halves what a screenshot-based reviewer sees.
9. **Positioning is inconsistent with what you want to sell.** The title says "AI Agent & RAG
   Developer", the H1 says "AI/ML Developer", and there is no mention of chatbots, bots or
   "AI automation" — the words most buyers type. "Experienced developer" isn't supported
   on-page by years, client logos, or outcomes.
10. **No social proof.** No testimonials, client names, reviews, ratings or case-study results
    on the site or in schema. This is the biggest trust gap for international clients. Your
    new ChatGPT-referred client is your first chance: ask for a written testimonial.

## Medium: optimisation opportunities

11. **Off-brand sections dilute the AI positioning:** "Side Hustles & Passions — My Poison",
    Gentle Estates (real estate) and equestrian sport. Keep one line about them on an About
    page; remove them from the hiring homepage.
12. **No pricing or engagement signals for international buyers:** no currency, rough
    budgets, time zone overlap, or "how we work across time zones" note.
13. **No llms.txt** (and the URL returns your homepage HTML). Add a plain-text summary of who
    you are, services, case studies and contact for AI assistants.
14. **OG image is served from the redirecting host** (`akshitsharma.dev/og-preview.png`
    returns a 15-byte redirect body). Some link-preview bots don't follow redirects, so
    WhatsApp/LinkedIn previews can break. Serve it from www, and compress it (212 KB PNG).
15. **Both GA4 (`gtag.js`) and GTM load.** Double-counting risk plus extra JS. Keep GTM only
    and fire GA4 from inside it.
16. **Unused font family:** Caveat is loaded from Google Fonts alongside Geist and Geist Mono.
17. **`keywords` meta tag** is ignored by Google and Bing and lists "best developer
    portfolio". Remove it.
18. **Accessibility:** colour-contrast failures, elements whose accessible name doesn't
    match their visible label, and touch targets too small on mobile.
19. **No web manifest or PNG apple-touch-icon** (both 404 → soft-404 to the homepage).
20. **Security headers:** HSTS, nosniff and frame-deny are present; add `Referrer-Policy`,
    `Permissions-Policy` and a Content-Security-Policy.
21. **A messy LinkedIn link** points at an overlay URL containing your internal profile ID;
    link the plain post or certificate instead.

## Off-site presence (where clients actually find freelancers)

22. **GitHub** (the first thing technical clients check): the bio doesn't mention AI agents
    or chatbots, no location, 12 of 17 own repos have no description, none has topics,
    2 stars total, and the flagship projects aren't pinned with READMEs that show outcomes.
23. **No listings on the platforms ranking for your services.** For "LangGraph / RAG / AI
    agent developer" searches, Fiverr gigs, Upwork Project Catalog items and Contra services
    rank on page one. You have an Upwork profile but no catalog projects.
24. **No Google Business Profile.** As a service business in Chandigarh you can appear in
    "AI developer near me" / "chatbot developer Chandigarh" map results.
25. **No content footprint:** no articles on LinkedIn, Medium, dev.to or Hashnode, and no
    talks or podcasts, so there's little for Google or AI assistants to cite about you.

## Action plan

**Week 1 (fixes, mostly code I can do)**
- Unify on `https://www.akshitsharma.dev` everywhere (canonical, og, sitemap, robots, JSON-LD).
- Return real 404s for unknown paths (Vercel `404.html` + only rewrite known routes).
- Cut the loader to first visit only (or under 1 s), drop GA4-or-GTM duplication, remove
  Caveat and the keywords tag, serve and compress the OG image from www.
- Fix duplicated headings and make reveal text visible by default (animate once).
- Add `llms.txt`, a web manifest, PNG touch icon and the missing security headers.
- Submit the sitemap in Google Search Console and Bing Webmaster Tools; request indexing.

**Weeks 2–3 (pages that can rank)**
- Pre-render the site (e.g. Vite SSG) so every page ships real HTML.
- Service pages: AI agent development, AI chatbot development, RAG / chat-with-your-docs,
  AI automation for businesses, each with process, pricing range, FAQ and FAQPage schema.
- One case-study page per project (problem → architecture → result), linked from Work.

  wording (time zones, currencies).

**Month 2 (authority and proof)**
- Collect 3–5 testimonials (start with the new client) and add Review schema.
- Publish 2 articles a month on topics clients search ("cost to build an AI chatbot",
  "RAG vs fine-tuning for support bots"), cross-posted to LinkedIn and dev.to.
- Clean up GitHub (bio, location, pinned repos, descriptions, topics, outcome READMEs).
- Create Upwork Project Catalog items, a Contra profile and 1–2 Fiverr gigs for AI agents
  and chatbots; set up a Google Business Profile.

**Ongoing**
- Track Search Console queries monthly; re-run Lighthouse after each release.
- Re-audit with `/seo audit https://www.akshitsharma.dev` (claude-seo, now installed) in a
